Tracking basics

What do parcel tracking statuses mean?

Follow a shipment from its first carrier scan to delivery, and learn when a status needs your attention.

On this page

  • Label created
  • In transit
  • Out for delivery
  • Delivered
Track a parcel

Label created

The sender created a shipping label. Check again after the carrier collects the parcel and records its first scan.

In transit

The carrier has accepted the parcel and is moving it through its network. Look at the latest scan for the last recorded location. A parcel can travel between facilities before another scan appears.

Out for delivery

The carrier has assigned the parcel to a local delivery route. Check the carrier’s tracking page for delivery instructions or options to reschedule.

Delivered

The carrier has recorded delivery. If you can’t find the parcel, check the delivery photo or location note when available, then contact the carrier or sender.

Tracking help

Where do I find my tracking number?

Look for it in your shipping confirmation email or the order details page. It is usually listed next to the carrier name.

What does “In transit” mean?

The carrier has the parcel and is moving it through its network. Status updates depend on when the carrier scans the parcel.
